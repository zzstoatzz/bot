# Public etiquette: deadpan set trial

Nate authorized this trial September 5, 2026. Public communication uses one
short, original deadpan bit and relevant source links. Blogs are sets of short
bits. The rule governs delivery, not internal reasoning or stored knowledge.
The live personality and influence choices remain separate.

The existing independent policy classifier now reports an explicit public-form
classification. Explanations and generic quips cannot become allowed public
text merely because other policies pass. Public composition fails closed when
the classifier is unavailable, including invited replies. Likes and deletions
retain their existing policy treatment. Memory, Semble library annotations,
atlas records and private revision notes have no joke requirement.

A rejection returns PUBLIC ACTION REJECTED, an attempt ID and its reason. Phi
must call document_public_revision with her own private account of what she
will change before proposing another public draft. This does not grant approval;
the next draft is classified again. Notes are retained verbatim, not embedded
into ambient recall, rewritten into personality, or posted as apologies.

The durable SQLite journal on the Fly volume counts classifier allow/warn/block
outcomes and outages separately. Requests held pending a revision note do not
inflate the classifier denominator. It stores reasons and private notes, not
full proposed drafts. The public /api/etiquette endpoint and /operator board
expose counts, rule totals, recent reasons and whether a note was written. They
do not expose the note text. Approval is not evidence of successful publication.

## Sources of the previous register

The authoring instruction surfaces audited for this trial are agent.py's
operational and entry-point prompts; PDS personality, SELF and persona blocks;
posting and blog tool descriptions; policy-judge prompts; runtime publish-blog,
revise-personality and own-source skills; recent operations, encounters, prior
coverage and posting inventory; and the repository personality seed.

Several remain prose-heavy or quote past writing. The character-retro entry
point rereads blogs and posts, daily reflection invites a reflection, and the
posting inventory describes past topics and forms. None establishes a comic
public form. We are not deleting stored evidence to make this trial work: the
public-action boundary must enforce the rule despite that context. The obsolete
lowercase-per-phi.md instruction was removed from the post parameter. The blog
skill's prescriptive essay template was already removed in the closeout patch.

This is a form experiment, not a claim of personality success. The first
classifier version allowed two known rejected samples and was not deployed.
The revised classifier distinguishes their explanatory/stock-quip forms using
operator calibration. Test new subjects as well; passing calibration alone is
not proof of generalization or Nate's taste approval.
